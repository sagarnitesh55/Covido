About Us

Welcome! 👋 The Covido App is here to make things simple when you’re not feeling your best and wondering, “Could this be COVID?”

What the App Does

It asks you a few quick yes or no questions about how you’re feeling - things like fever, cough, or if you’ve travelled overseas. Based on your answers, it gives you a quick prediction of whether your symptoms might be linked to COVID-19.

Why You’ll Like It


  1. ✅ Super quick – no long forms, just yes/no questions.
  2. ✅ Easy to use – anyone can try it, no medical jargon.
  3. ✅ Helpful – gives you a starting point to decide if you should get tested or talk to a doctor.


A Little Heads-Up

This app is just a helper tool, not a doctor. It’s meant to give you guidance, not a medical diagnosis. If you’re feeling really unwell - especially with serious symptoms like trouble breathing or high fever - please reach out to a healthcare professional right away. Stay safe, stay healthy ❤️
